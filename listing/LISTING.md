This mod is designed specifically for the [Dungeon Train modpack](https://modrinth.com/modpack/bEFyz3ji) / [mod](https://modrinth.com/mod/dungeon-train), but can be used independently.

## What it does

[Sable](https://modrinth.com/mod/sable) lets you build moving ships, trains and other structures. Out of the box, a mob can find its way around the one structure it is standing on, but it can't plan a route from the ground onto one, off it again, or across to the next.

Sable Mob Paths fills that gap:

- **Boarding and getting off.** Mobs chasing you, or anything else they want to reach, walk onto a moving structure and step off it again.
- **Crossing between structures.** Mobs walk from one structure to the next, such as from carriage to carriage along a train.
- **Villagers that live on board.** Villagers on a structure use the job sites, beds and bells on that same structure. They work, farm, sleep, breed and spawn iron golems there.
- **Raids that follow the ship.** A Bad Omen on board starts a raid that stays with the structure as it moves.

## What it doesn't do

- A villager won't claim a job site or bed on a different structure from the one it is on.
- Amphibious mobs, such as frogs, don't cross onto structures.
- Paths on a tilted structure are approximate. It assumes structures stay level.
- Mobs don't jump a full block up onto a deck. Give a raised deck a slab, stairs or a ramp to walk up.

## Setup

- Needs Sable 2.0.5 or newer, on NeoForge 1.21.1.
- Install it on the server, or in your game for singleplayer.
- No config, commands, items or blocks. It just works.
- It doesn't send anything over the network.

To check it is running, look for `[SableMobPaths]` in `latest.log`.
